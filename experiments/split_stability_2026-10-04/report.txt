  2. HOW OFTEN DOES EACH PUBLISHED CONCLUSION HOLD?
==============================================================================

  (a)  Retention: post-hoc learned router minus entropy gate
       across splits   median +1.69   [-0.91, +3.87]
       thesis reports  +1.10  [-1.30, +3.50]  from the single drawn split
       The two ranges agree closely, so the published split was
       representative. The interval still contains zero, so the
       conclusion of no reliable retention advantage stands.

  (b)  Carbon: the thesis claim about WHERE the router's advantage comes from
       prompt-only router (skips the discarded small run) minus entropy
         median +14.59  [+5.46, +24.25]   positive in 100.0% of splits
       post-hoc router (pays for the small run) minus entropy
         median +0.86  [-10.80, +10.93]   positive in 54.5% of splits
       This is the central claim of Ablation D and it holds in every
       split: the carbon advantage belongs to deciding early, not to
       routing better. Charge the router for the discarded run and it
       disappears.

  (c)  Entropy gate against the static small tier
       retention        median +2.61  [+1.19, +4.53]
       carbon reduction median -30.04  [-38.61, -22.75]
       The gate buys a little quality at a large carbon cost in every
       split. Neither option dominates, which is the trade-off the
       thesis reports rather than a win for routing.
